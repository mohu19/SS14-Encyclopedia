#Alert Levels

Alert levels represent the danger that is currently directed towards the station. There are 7 alert levels you should look out for.

<span style="color:#00a933">Code Green</span>: Standard alert at the start of the shift, no danger.

<span style="color:#2a6099">Code Blue</span>: A danger has been shown to be present on the station

<span style="color:#ff0000">Code Red</span>: There is active danger towards the station crew.

<span style="color:#ec9ba4">Code Gamma</span>: There is a threat of total station destruction.

<span style="color:#8d281e">Code Delta</span>: The onboard nuclear device has been armed, station destruction is imminent.

<span style="color:#acb20c">Code Yellow</span>: There is an atmospheric or structural threat to the station.

<span style="color:#ff8119">Code Orange</span>: The station has taken critical structural damage, and is unlikely to recover.

<span style="color:#8d1d75">Code Violet</span>: There is a biohazard threat on board the station.
