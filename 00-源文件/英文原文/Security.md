# Security
Ensuring the safety of both station and crew is number one for <span style="color:#cb0000">Security</span>. They are ready for any situation, and <span style="color:#cb0000">Security</span> are on hand to maintain order and control crowds.

They face [textlink="Syndicate Agents" link="Traitors"], [textlink="Nuclear Operatives" link="NuclearOperatives"], [textlink="Zombies" link="Zombies"] and unruly staff.

## Personnel
<span style="color:#cb0000">Security</span>'s staff is made up of Security Cadets, Security Officers, and the Detective. <span style="color:#cb0000">Security</span> is run by the Warden and Head of Security.

## Flashes
<span style="color:#a4885c">Flashes</span> are an effective tool for dispersing crowds and apprehending particularly evasive targets, with a large area of effect blinding all those without protection.
It is worth noting that flashes can be both used in a large area (<span style="color:yellow">Z</span> hotkey) and if you attack somebody with it while <span style="color:red">harm mode</span> is active. Important to note that localized flashes last <span style="color:yellow">2.5</span> times as long as large area ones.
<span style="color:#cb0000">Security</span> and <span style="color:#a4885c">some</span> other members of staff have eye protection from flashbangs and flashes.
