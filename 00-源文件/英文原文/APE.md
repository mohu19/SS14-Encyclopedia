# A.P.E.

The Anomalous Particle Emitter is a machine used to interface with anomalies. It is the primary way of manipulating the stats of an anomaly.


[[实体:MachineAPE]]


The A.P.E. can emit four regular kinds of anomalous particles:
Delta, Epsilon, Sigma, and Zeta.

The A.P.E can also emit a fifth type: Nullspace stabilizer particles. These higher-energy particles are needed to quell specialized anomalies.

Each particle type has certain effects:

- <span style="color:crimson">Danger:</span> Increases the severity of an anomaly.
- <span style="color:plum">Unstable:</span> Increases the instability of an anomaly.
- <span style="color:goldenrod">Containment:</span> Increases anomaly stability at the cost of its health - if an anomaly is struck by too many containment particles, it will collapse, leaving behind an anomaly core.
- <span style="color:#6b75fa">Transformation:</span> Increases anomaly severity with a chance of changing its behavior.

Which particle type corresponds to each effect is unique and can be learned by scanning the anomaly.

The A.P.E. can also be locked by anyone with science access, which prevents it from being deconstructed, turned on or off, or having the particle type switched.
