# Minor Antagonists

  Minor Antagonists are ghost-controlled roles that give dead people a second chance to interact (and cause chaos!) around the station. They are spawned by random events.

  # Paradox Clone

  
    [[实体:ParadoxCloneDummy]]
  

  A perfect copy of a random crewmember that was thrown into our universe by a space-time anomaly. To fix the paradox and survive they have to find and kill their original counterpart.

  Both clones are the "real" clone and all other crew has memories that match the history of both clones.
  Because of this, crew should treat their memory as unreliable and trust that both clones are telling the truth.
  Additionally, both clones believe they are the original.

  They have no special abilities, but are identical in appearance, DNA, fingerprints, clothing, equipment and IDs, making it hard to figure out who is the original and who is the clone... unless you know your fellow crewmembers well!

  # Revenant

  
    [[实体:MobRevenant]]
  

  Revenants are ethereal beings made of ectoplasm that haunt the crew and steal <span style="color:#8f63e6">life essence</span> from them.

  Revenant is allowed to destroy burn chambers or space the station to obtain souls.

  - Your essence is your very own **life force**. Taking damage reduces your essence.
  - Essence is also spent to use **abilities**.
  - To gain essence you first **inspect** various souls for their worth. More worthy souls grant more essence. Then you must **siphon** it either when the victim is crit, dead or asleep. All of this is done with <span style="color:yellow">**[keybind="Use"]**</span>.
  - **Stolen essence** is used to purchase new abilities to incapacitate the crew easier.
  - You are **vulnerable** to attacks when you siphon a soul or use an ability. You can tell when you are vulnerable when you have a <span style="color:#8f63e6">*Corporeal*</span> status effect.

  # Rat King

  
    [[实体:MobRatServant]]
    [[实体:MobLargeRatServant]]
    
    [[实体:MobLargeRatServant]]
    [[实体:MobRatServant]]
  

  Rat kings are abnormally large rodents whose primary goals are <span style="color:yellow">raising huge rat armies</span> and eating equally huge amounts of food.

  Rat kings are antags, but they may make deals with the crew to further their goals.
  Rat kings should <span style="color:red">NOT</span> go out of their way to protect the crew unless it is absolutely necessary.
  If the crew stands in the way of a Rat King getting food the Rat King is aware that crew are *emergency food*.

  - You have several abilities that come at the cost of your **hunger.**
  - One such ability is <span style="color:yellow">*Raise Army*</span>, which summons a **Rat Servant**. You can **command** your servants to stay in place, follow you, attack a target of your choice or attack indiscriminately.
  - You can conjure a cloud of <span style="color:#77b58e">ammonia</span> using <span style="color:purple">*Rat King's Domain*</span>. This purple gas is mildly poisonous to humans but heals rats.
  - <span style="color:#9daa98">*Rummage*</span> through a disposal unit by using the context menu. Use this to find **scraps of food** to grow your army.
  - Besides your abilities, **you are stronger than most other animals.** You and your servants are capable of speech and you're both small enough to fit under airlocks and tables.
  - **Your underlings are still very fragile!** Beware of spacings, explosions and the business end of a melee weapon.

  # Space Dragon

  
    [[实体:MobCarp]]
    [[实体:CarpRift]]
    [[实体:MobDragon]]
    [[实体:CarpRift]]
    [[实体:MobCarp]]
  

  Space dragons are giant extradimensional fish that can <span style="color:red">eat crew</span> and create <span style="color:#4b7fcc">carp rifts.</span>

  Space Dragons are considered a priority threat and command should not make deals with them.

  - Dragons have powerful jaws that allow them to <span style="color:red">*Devour*</span> infrastructure such as doors, windows, and walls.
  - They can also heal themselves by using <span style="color:red">*Devour*</span> on crew members that have gone unconscious or have died.
  - Your <span style="color:orange">*Dragon's Breath*</span> is a **flaming projectile** which travels a straight flight path, exploding multiple times and igniting things along the way. Try not to hit your carps with this!
  - You have the ability to <span style="color:#4b7fcc">*Summon a Carp Rift*</span>. Rifts periodically spawn **space carp** and charge up energy over a period of 5 minutes, **which is when it's vulnerable.** After 5 minutes, it becomes invulnerable and will continue summoning carp as long as the dragon lives.
  - You'll suffer a **temporary debilitating feedback effect** if one of your rifts are destroyed before it's done charging.
  - If a period of five minutes passes since your spawn or since the last time a rift was charging, **you'll disappear.**
  - Dragons who have the maximum of 3 fully charged rifts will **never disappear**, but they are still mortal.

  # Slimes and Spiders

  
    [[实体:MobAdultSlimesBlueAngry]]
    [[实体:MobGiantSpiderAngry]]
  

  Slimes and spiders have no remarkable features, but will <span style="color:cyan">infest the station</span> from time to time regardless. Both will give chase and attack anything they see.

  Hostile Slimes and Spiders are driven by hunger and territorial aggression, they are not station pets.
  Clown Spiders are allowed to protect assumed clowns.

  - Slimes may **deal extra cellular or poison damage**, based upon their color. Water hurts them just as it would hurt a Laspi. 
  - Spiders have a venomous bite and can **create webs** that are hard to move through, but are easily destroyed with a blade. They can also pry open doors, windoors, and airlocks.
