# Engineering
  <span style="color:#f39f27">Engineering</span> is a combination of [textlink="construction" link="Construction"] work, [textlink="repair" link="ExpandingRepairingStation"] work, maintaining a death machine that happens to produce [textlink="power" link="Power"], and making sure the station contains [textlink="breathable air" link="Atmospherics"].

  ## Personnel
  <span style="color:#f39f27">Engineering</span>'s staff is made up of Technical Assistants, Station Engineers and Atmospheric Technicians.
  Engineering is run by the Chief Engineer.
