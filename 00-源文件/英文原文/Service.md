# Service
<span style="color:#9fed58">Service</span> keeps the station clean, efficient and sane so that all other departments can do their jobs better and keep their staff happy. They also end up doing most of the paperwork on the station.

## Personnel
<span style="color:#9fed58">Service</span>'s staff is made up of Service Workers, Chefs, Botanists, Bartenders, Janitors, Chaplains, Clowns, Mimes, Musicians, Lawyers, Librarians and Assistants. Service is run by the Head of Personnel.

## Working Together
<span style="color:#9fed58">Service</span>, despite being the most diverse, tends to be the most <span style="color:#a4885c">interlinked</span> department by far.
Most <span style="color:#9fed58">Service</span> jobs require <span style="color:#a4885c">cooperation</span> between workers.
Chefs often need ingredients that only botanists can grow efficiently. The bartender may request bananas that only clowns carry in abundance.
The lawyer may invite the mime to play charades to convince the judge that their client is innocent.

A lot of <span style="color:#a4885c">collaboration</span> is necessary within and without <span style="color:#9fed58">Service</span>. Often times, <span style="color:#9fed58">Service</span> is required by other departments, so be on your toes.
