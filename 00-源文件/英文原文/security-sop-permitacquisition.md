# Permit Acquisition

- 1.\n
Both the Warden and Head of Security are cleared to approve permits for equipment that they themselves are allowed to use.\n\n
1.1\n
This should not be done without proper cause.\n\n
1.2\n
Captured enemy equipment, such as from the Syndicate, may be approved via Central Command decree if requested, but not on their own authority.
- 2.\n
The approval of permits is a privilege, not a right, and neither the Warden nor the Head of Security is obligated to approve any permit.
- 3.\n
Copies of all permits must be kept in the Warden's office.
- 4.\n
Permits are invalid if not kept on their owner's person.
