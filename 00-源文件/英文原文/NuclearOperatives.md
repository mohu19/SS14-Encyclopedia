# Nuclear Operatives

  
    <span style="color:#999999">*"OPERATIVES STANDBY. YOUR OBJECTIVES ARE SIMPLE.*</span>
  
  
    <span style="color:#999999">*DELIVER THE PAYLOAD AND GET OUT BEFORE THE PAYLOAD DETONATES. BEGIN MISSION."*</span>
  
  
    [[实体:PosterContrabandEnlistGorlex]]
  

  You've just been chosen to be a nuclear operative for the <span style="color:#ff0000">Syndicate</span>. You have one goal, to blow up one of Nanotrasen's finest vessels with a nuclear fission explosive.

  ## Operatives
  Your team will be made up of several different roles, which are:
  - **Operatives**, standard soldiers who can take up the grunt work.
  - The **Corpsman** who works with reagents to make medicines or chemical weapons.
  - The **Commander** who draws up the plan and leads the team.

  
    [[实体:ClothingOuterHardsuitSyndie]]
    [[实体:ClothingOuterHardsuitSyndieMedic]]
    [[实体:ClothingOuterHardsuitSyndieCommander]]
  

  If you have been selected as the Corpsman or Commander but don't feel ready, one of your teammates can take over your responsibilities.

  ## The Disk

  Each operative starts with a <span style="color:cyan">pinpointer</span>, which you should turn on and keep with you at all times. It will always point to the <span style="color:cyan">nuclear authentication disk.</span>
  The disk belongs to the <span style="color:yellow">Captain</span>, who will almost always have it on them. Follow the direction of the arrow on the pinpointer to the disk, and *do whatever it takes to get it*.

  ## Getting to the Station
  You've got the plan, you've got the gear. Now, execute.

  Grab a **jetpack** from your armory and go with your other operatives to your <span style="color:cyan">shuttle</span>.
  Among other things you may need, you'll find an **IFF Computer** on the shuttle. It allows you to hide from other ships and mass scanners when *Show IFF* and *Show Vessel* are toggled off.

  When everyone is ready, FTL to the station and fly to it with a jetpack. Don't forget the code, your pinpointers and the nuke if you're taking it.

  
    [[实体:JetpackBlack]]
    [[实体:ComputerIFFSyndicate]]
  

  ## The Nuke & Arming Procedure
  You have a paper with the <span style="color:cyan">nuclear authentication codes</span> on your shuttle. **Check to see if the nuke ID matches the one on your shuttle.** If it doesn't, you'll be arming the station's nuke instead.

  - Obtain the **nuclear authentication disk** and insert it into the nuke.
  - Type in the **nuclear authentication code** and press "**E**" on the keypad to Enter.
  - **To begin the self-destruct sequence, press <span style="color:#EB2D3A">ARM</span>**. After 300 seconds, the nuke will explode.
  - **Defend the nuke**, even if it's at the cost of your lives! The mission requirements do not include your return.
  - It takes 30 seconds for someone to **disarm** the nuke. Re-arming it is possible, but the chance of mission success drops if you let it happen.
  - Should the nuke be re-armed, the timer will start from where it left off.

  
    [[实体:NuclearBomb]]
  

  ## Winning Conditions

  The <span style="color:#00ff00">victor</span> of the round is announced on the round end screen, as well as the scale of their victory.

  **<span style="color:red">Syndicate Major Victory!</span>**
  - The nuke detonates on the station.
  - The crew escapes on evac, but the nuke is still armed.
  - The nuke is armed and delivered to Central Command on the evac shuttle.

  **<span style="color:red">Syndicate Minor Victory</span>**
  - The crew escapes on evac, but every operative survives.
  - The crew escapes and some operatives die, but the crew loses the disk.

  **<span style="color:#999999">Neutral Victory</span>**
  - The nuke detonates off the station.

  **<span style="color:green">Crew Minor Victory</span>**
  - The crew escapes on evac with the disk and some operatives die.

  **<span style="color:green">Crew Major Victory!</span>**
  - All operatives die.
  - The crew blow up the nuclear operative outpost with the nuke.
