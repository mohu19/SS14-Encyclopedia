# Command
The heads of each department come together to form the <span style="color:#1b67a5">Command</span> department, a force to be reckoned with and the handlers of the station. They usually have access to special equipment and their own rooms. They are often wanted dead.

## Personnel
<span style="color:#1b67a5">Command</span> is made up of the heads of each department, being the <span style="color:#9fed58">Head of Personnel</span>, the <span style="color:#cb0000">Head of Security</span>, the <span style="color:#5b97bc">Chief Medical Officer</span>, the <span style="color:#f39f27">Chief Engineer</span>, the <span style="color:#c96dbf">Research Director</span>, the <span style="color:#b18644">Quartermaster</span> and above all else, the <span style="color:#1b67a5">Captain</span>.

## Taking Charge
Congratulations on your promotion! Besides all the extra paperwork, your new <span style="color:#a4885c">responsibilities</span> include running your department, making changes and choosing priorities, keeping personnel and equipment safe as well as slacking off while your cronies do all the work.

Don't forget, you still have a job! Your fancy tools don't earn you a day off, you still have to fill in for any staffing shortages, helping out struggling workers and teaching the newbies.

You might get away with just sitting around and letting people resent you, but it's wiser to get active and work alongside them.
Remember to move your workers around to have them do what they like doing or what they do best.

## Your Locker

To deal with your new soul-crushing tasks, every head is given special items to lead your subordinates. Most of your new shiny tools can be found in your <span style="color:#a4885c">locker</span>.
  
    [[实体:LockerCaptain]]
  

Your <span style="color:#a4885c">remote</span> is very simple but profoundly underestimated. A small but mighty force, your remote can open and close <span style="color:#a4885c">airlocks</span>, spin the <span style="color:#a4885c">bolts</span> to lock them, or toggle <span style="color:#a4885c">emergency access</span>. It can do this from a distance.
  
    [[实体:DoorRemoteCommand]]
  
The remote influences where people move around. Heads can lock personnel out of dangerous areas or let people in to grab something really quickly.

You also have access to your very own <span style="color:#1b67a5">Command</span> channel, like every other department. However, yours is particularly special as the people who can hear you have <span style="color:#a4885c">entire departments</span> at their fingertips.
Use this to communicate with other heads and be a representative of what your workers want or need.

The <span style="color:#1b67a5">Captain</span> gets access to the <span style="color:#a4885c">master encryption key</span>, letting them tune into every radio channel on the station. This is extremly valuable, as you can coordinate between departments yourself.
<span style="color:#a4885c">Don't waste this vital advantage!</span>
