# Science
<span style="color:#c96dbf">Science</span>, often called Research and Development, is a job consisting of generating research points to unlock new technologies and using said technologies to produce new items at lathes to help out the station.

## Personnel
<span style="color:#c96dbf">Science</span>'s staff is made up of Research Assistants and Scientists. <span style="color:#c96dbf">Science</span> is run by the Research Director.

## Technologies
  
    [[实体:ComputerResearchAndDevelopment]]
    [[实体:ResearchAndDevelopmentServer]]
  
The most important thing inside your department is the R&D server, which stores unlocked technologies, and the R&D computer, which allows you to unlock <span style="color:#a4885c">technologies</span>.

Each technology costs <span style="color:#a4885c">Research Points</span> and unlocks recipes at lathes. Some technologies will also have prerequisites you have to unlock before you can research them.

## Tiers
Each Discipline tree has up to 3 tiers of technologies. At the start the only tier that is open and available for you is tier 1.

In order to progress further, you have to research up to <span style="color:brown">75%</span> of the current tier to move to the next one. So in order to unlock T2 researches you need to research 75% of T1.

Now <span style="color:green">Tier 3</span> is where it gets interesting. If you research a T3 tech, you <span style="color:brown">lock out</span> T3 techs from other Discipline trees. That is, unless you can find some other way around it... you should probably ask your <span style="color:purple">Research Director</span>.
