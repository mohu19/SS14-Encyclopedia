# NanoTrasen Employee SOP

This Standard Operating Procedure is to help guide NanoTrasen Personnel which are aboard the station. NanoTrasen Personnel are considered part of the crew, however are not employed by Central Command. These crew members however have no official commanding power on the station outside of what is explicitly stated in their SOP.
