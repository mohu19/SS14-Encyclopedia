# Cargo

  The Supply department, also known as <span style="color:cyan">Cargo</span>, supplies the station with necessary resources, such as fuel and raw materials, and comfort resources, such as lottery tickets and plushies.

  They raise the <span style="color:cyan">spesos</span> (the currency of the station) for this through many methods, such as taking bounties, selling miscellaneous items, and salvaging, allowing them to purchase a wide variety of equipment from off-station for any crew member's needs. It is Cargo's job to fulfill the needs of the rest of the station.

  ## Personnel

  Cargo is made up of <span style="color:cyan">Cargo Technicians</span> and <span style="color:cyan">Salvage Specialists</span> and is led by the <span style="color:cyan">Quartermaster</span>.

  ## Making Money

  The main struggle of any Cargo department is not having enough money.

  Cargo makes their money by selling things at the <span style="color:cyan">Trading Station</span>. Almost everything on the station has economic value that you can sell, as seen via the <span style="color:cyan">appraisal tool</span>. That value then further increases when you sell it as part of a [textlink="bounty" link="CargoBounties"].

  
    [[实体:AppraisalTool]]
    [[实体:CargoPallet]]
    [[实体:CrateGenericSteel]]
  

  Upon finding something valuable, bring it to the <span style="color:cyan">Cargo Bay</span>. If it fits inside a <span style="color:cyan">Crate</span>, find an empty crate and put the items in it.

  If the <span style="color:cyan">Cargo Shuttle</span> is away, you can put all the items you want to sell onto the <span style="color:cyan">Cargo Pallets</span>. Once the shuttle has docked at the station, you load the crates and everything else to be sold onto it.

  
    [[实体:ComputerPalletConsole]]
    [[实体:CargoPalletSell]]
    [[实体:ComputerCargoOrders]]
  

  After the shuttle is loaded, fly it to the Trade Station. There, move the sellable items onto the green <span style="color:cyan">Cargo Selling Pallets</span>, and then go to the <span style="color:cyan">Cargo Sale Computer</span> and click *"Sell"*.

  Now, you can take those spesos and bring them back to the station and deposit them into the <span style="color:cyan">Cargo Request Computer</span>.

  You can also make even more money by completing [textlink="bounties" link="CargoBounties"] or selling valuable items from [textlink="salvage" link="Salvage"].

  ## Ordering

  
    [[实体:ComputerCargoOrders]]
  

  Every order begins at a <span style="color:cyan">Cargo Request Computer</span>. First and foremost, making an order requires that Cargo has enough spesos. If Cargo doesn't have the money, get some.

  It is also the sole discretion of Cargo to fulfill any given request. If Cargo is tight on spesos, they are free to deny requests that are not immediately necessary.

  To make an order, click the name of whatever is requested, provide the recipient, type an appropriate reason for why they need it, and then press "Approve" on the order.

  Sometimes a public Cargo Request Computer exists, in which case all you need to do is approve orders that people make themselves.

  
    [[实体:CargoPalletBuy]]
    [[实体:PaperCargoInvoice]]
  

  Then, to collect the order, it must be transported back to the station from the <span style="color:cyan">Trading Station</span>.

  In the Trading Station, on the <span style="color:cyan">Cargo Buying Pallets</span>, is a crate holding that order. Load it onto the shuttle and fly it back.

  Once it is at the Cargo Dock, you can read the <span style="color:cyan">Cargo Invoice</span> label on it to find out the intended recipient. Finally, deliver it to them.

  ## Piloting

  Orders are delivered to the station via the orbiting <span style="color:cyan">Trade Station</span> and are transported to the main station by the <span style="color:cyan">Cargo Shuttle[color]. The shuttle does not fly itself, so it must either be piloted in the shuttle or remotely.

  
    [[实体:ComputerShuttleCargo]]
  

  To fly the shuttle, you must first find the [color=cyan]Cargo Shuttle Console</span>. One is inside the Cargo Bay, and the other is inside the Cargo Shuttle.

  After interacting with the computer, there should be a navigation view from the shuttle. Here are the steps for piloting the shuttle to and from the Trading Station:

  - First, switch to the <span style="color:yellow">"DOCK"</span> screen and <span style="color:yellow">undock</span> any airlocks between the shuttle and the station.
  - Now, with the shuttle disconnected, you can pilot it. *Remember that you are flying in space and momentum is conserved*.
  - The controls are <span style="color:yellow">[keybind="ShuttleStrafeUp"]</span> and <span style="color:yellow">[keybind="ShuttleStrafeDown"]</span> for strafing up and down, <span style="color:yellow">[keybind="ShuttleStrafeLeft"]</span> and <span style="color:yellow">[keybind="ShuttleStrafeRight"]</span> for strafing left and right, and <span style="color:yellow">[keybind="ShuttleRotateLeft"]</span> and <span style="color:yellow">[keybind="ShuttleRotateRight"]</span> for turning left and right. To brake, press <span style="color:yellow">[keybind="ShuttleBrake"]</span>.
  - Then pilot the shuttle to the <span style="color:cyan">Trade Station</span>.
  - Once you are there, <span style="color:yellow">dock</span> to the airlocks and you are done.
