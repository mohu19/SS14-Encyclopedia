# Abductors

  
    <span style="color:#999999">*" ..."*</span>
  
  Abductors are an alien society set on cataloging and experimenting on the sectors personnel.
  Its important to note: Abductors, unlike most antagonists are <span style="color:red">NOT set on killing people</span> and are exclusively interested in completing their experiments.

  Abductors must make every attempt to leave no trace and avoid detection while doing their task. Whether it be abductions or retrieval of emergency supplies.
  Similarly, abductors must never intervene in events on the station if at all avoidable, even if this means the end of the experiment, as otherwise said experiment's impurity would make it useless for the mothership's research.

  Abductors do not possess the ability to speak and therefore communicate through more... mime-like methods.
  They do however have a hivemind: "+q"

  Abductors are <span style="color:red">NOT</span> to steal any items from crew or the station.
  However, there are rare exceptions. Abductors may steal in order to:
  - Repair their shuttle
  - Get more meds to heal due to *somehow* running out
  - Get more surgical tools if they *somehow* lost theirs.
  - Stealing stun baton as a solo abductor or when a wonderprod cannot be obtained.
  - Other very specific emergency cases where theft HAS to be done to prevent issues in what abductors normally do.

  <span style="color:red">AHELP</span> if you are unsure.

  ##Abduction
  Abductors begin on their ship somewhere near the station. Using the <span style="color:#a149bf">"Human Observation Console"</span> you can seek out victims who are alone and <span style="color:lightblue">warp</span> near them to begin abduction.

  The wonderprod is the primary tool of an Abductor. It has four modes you will use for abduction:
  - <span style="color:yellow">{Stun}</span> Stuns the victim for roughly 5 seconds.
  - <span style="color:pink">{Restrain}</span> binds the victims hands in weak cables.
  - <span style="color:blue">{Sleep}</span> Induces a sleep-like state in the victim.
  - <span style="color:red">{Harm}</span> Burns the target, dealing damage. **ONLY USE IF YOU MUST.**

  Once a target is immobilized you can use the *gizmo* to implant them with a nano tracker, return to your ship using the <span style="color:lightblue">return action</span>, then use the gizmo on your <span style="color:#a149bf">Abductor Console</span>.

  ##Experimentation
  Once you have linked the most recent nano tracker to the console, select **{ATTRACT}** to warp your victim to your ship.

  Now that you have your victim aboard, its recommended you:
  - Buckle them to your surgical table.
  - Change out their cuffs for a higher grade of handcuffs provided on the ship.
  - Remove any of their belongings that could damage you or the secrecy of the experiment.
  - Use your vast surgical knowledge to replace their heart with a gland.
  - Give them back their primitive technology.

  Finally, drag the victim whom you have completed your experiment on into the <span style="color:#a149bf">Experimentator</span> to confirm the results of the experiment on the <span style="color:#a149bf">Abductor Console</span> and send them back where you found them.

  ##Getting rid of an Abductor
  Like [textlink="Changelings" link="Changelings"], Abductors are not protected by [textlink="space law" link="SpaceLaw"] and are able to be executed freely.

  Also, an abductor's ship is a physical object in the world, anyone with access to a space-faring ship such as [textlink="salvage" link="Salvage"] can bump into them in space and dismantle their ship.
