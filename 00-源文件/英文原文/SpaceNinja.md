# Space Ninja

  
    <span style="color:#999999">*"Spider of the night, silent is my bite."*</span>
  

  You are an elite mercenary that <span style="color:#66FF00">The Spider Clan</span> has sent to wreak all kinds of havoc on the station. You are equipped to keep it silent-but-deadly.

  Whether you get bloody or stay out of trouble, your discipline has taught you that *your objectives must be at least attempted*. For honor!

  ## Standard Equipment
  You begin implanted with a <span style="color:cyan">death acidifier</span>, so if you are captured or KIA all your precious equipment is kept out of enemy hands.

  Your bag is full of **tools** and a **survival box**. You also carry a <span style="color:cyan">jetpack</span> and a <span style="color:cyan">pinpointer</span> that will lead you to the station.

  ## Your Suit

  
    [[实体:ClothingEyesVisorNinja]]
    [[实体:ClothingOuterSuitSpaceNinja]]
    [[实体:ClothingShoesSpaceNinja]]
  

  Your single most important item is <span style="color:cyan">your suit</span>, without it none of your abilities would work.
  Your suit requires power to function, which is supplied by its **internal battery**. It can be replaced, and *high capacity batteries* mean a *highly effective ninja*.

  **You can recharge your internal battery directly** by using <span style="color:cyan">your gloves</span>. You can see the current charge by examining the suit or checking the nifty battery alert on your screen.

  Your outfit also includes <span style="color:cyan">special boots</span> that keep you agile, upright and grounded without using energy. You've also got flash protection thanks to your <span style="color:cyan">visor</span>.

  ## Ninja Gloves

  
    [[实体:ClothingHandsGlovesSpaceNinja]]
  

  <span style="color:#66FF00">These bad boys are your bread and butter.</span> They are made from **insulated nanomachines** to allow you to gracefully break into restricted areas without leaving behind fingerprints.

  You have an action to toggle gloves. When the gloves are turned on, they allow you to use **special abilities**, which are triggered by interacting with things with an empty hand and with combat mode disabled.

  Your glove abilities include:
  - Emagging doors and airlocks.
  - Draining power from **transformers** such as APCs, substations, or SMESes (higher voltage transformers drain more efficiently).
  - Applying an electrical shock to a mob, stunning and slightly damaging them.
  - Tampering with **station equipment** to complete <span style="color:#66FF00">objectives</span>.

  ## Energy Katana

  
    [[实体:EnergyKatana]]
  

  **You have sworn yourself to the sword and refuse to use firearms.** Luckily, you've got a pretty cool sword.

  Your <span style="color:cyan">energy katana</span> hurts plenty and can be **recalled at will** at the cost of suit power. The farther away it is from you, the more energy required to recall it.

  While in hand you may also <span style="color:cyan">teleport</span> to anywhere that you can see, **including through windows**. The charges required to do this regenerate slowly, so keep a charge or two spare in case you need a quick getaway.

  ## Spider Clan Charge

  
    [[实体:SpiderCharge]]
  

  This is a <span style="color:cyan">modified C-4 explosive</span> which can be found in your pocket. Creates a large explosion but **must be armed in your target area** as it is one of your objectives.

  It can't be activated manually, so simply plant it on a wall near your assigned target. Choose wisely, it can't be unstuck once planted.

  ## Objectives

  - Download \[9-13\] research nodes: Use your gloves on an R&D server with a number of unlocked technologies. You may have to unlock technologies yourself if there aren't enough to steal already.
  - Doorjack \[15-40\] doors on the station: Use your gloves to emag a number of doors.
  - Detonate the spider clan charge: Plant your spider clan charge at the specified location and watch it go boom!
  - Call in a [textlink="threat" link="MinorAntagonists"]: Use your gloves on a communications console.
  - Set everyone to wanted: Use your gloves on a criminal records console.
  - Survive: Don't die.
