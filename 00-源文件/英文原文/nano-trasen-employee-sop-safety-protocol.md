# Safety Protocol

1. As a direct hire of NanoTrasen, your safety is paramount. <span style="color:#ff0000">Code Red</span> or above, all NanoTrasen personnel should seek shelter, with exception of the BSO who should continue their duties.

2. In the event of a <span style="color:#ec9ba4">Code Gamma</span>, all NanoTrasen Personnel are to communicate and gather on the Bridge and await ERT support.

3. In the event of contract terminations, all NanoTrasen Personnel will be escorted off station by a NanoTrasen marine squad.
