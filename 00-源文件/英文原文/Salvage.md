# Salvage

  <span style="color:cyan">Salvage</span> is one of Cargo's key, but also most dangerous, sources of income. Salvage extracts money from <span style="color:cyan">Space Debris</span> and the enigmatic <span style="color:cyan">Mining Asteroid</span>.

  While the void of space is full of terrors, with proper preparation and coordination any Salvage Team can make a lot of money for Cargo.

  ## Preparations

  Before leaving the station, ensure you are prepared with all the required equipment. Otherwise, you will likely die before seeing any profits!

  
    [[实体:SuitStorageSalv]]
    [[实体:LockerSalvageSpecialist]]
    [[实体:VendingMachineSalvage]]
  

  Everything needed before leaving the station is provided inside Salvage's starting room. While Science can research better weapons and equipment, it is best to be equipped with your starting gear rather than nothing.

  
    [[实体:ClothingOuterHardsuitSpatio]]
    [[实体:ClothingShoesBootsMag]]
    [[实体:VendingMachineTankDispenserEVA]]
  

  The most basic equipment you need is the EVA setup.

  - <span style="color:cyan">Spationaut Hardsuit</span>: This keeps you alive in space. *Remember to activate the helmet so you can breathe and turn on its built-in flashlight*.
  - <span style="color:cyan">Magboots</span>: These provide you with gravity when exploring space debris while also making you slip-proof.
  - <span style="color:cyan">Oxygen/Nitrogen</span>: Depending on your species, you will need <span style="color:cyan">Nitrogen</span> (slimes & vox) or <span style="color:cyan">Oxygen</span> (every other species). The <span style="color:cyan">Gas Tank Dispenser</span> has all the tanks needed, so stock up.

  
    [[实体:WeaponProtoKineticAccelerator]]
  

  All Salvage Specialists must be armed, in case they need to defend themselves from hostiles.

  - <span style="color:cyan">Proto-Kinetic Accelerator</span>: This gun has infinite ammunition, allowing you to slowly mine and fly through space. Science can print upgrades to make it hit harder, shoot farther, and cycle faster.

  
    [[实体:Pickaxe]]
    [[实体:OreBag]]
  

  While you can technically mine and gather ore with just a PKA and a Backpack, Salvagers start with more specialized gear for the task.

  - <span style="color:cyan">Pickaxe</span>: This is required for mining at any reasonable speed when wielded. It also works as a handy melee weapon!
  - <span style="color:cyan">Ore Bag</span>: When equipped in your belt slot, this magnetizes nearby ore and automatically picks it up for you.

  
    [[实体:FireExtinguisher]]
    [[实体:WeaponGrapplingGun]]
    [[实体:SalvagePDA]]
    [[实体:ComputerRadar]]
  

  Although you can acquire more advanced forms of travel, you'll begin with more humble methods.

  - <span style="color:cyan">Fire Extinguisher</span>: The poor man's Jetpack. While in space, you can use a Fire Extinguisher's knockback to propel yourself in the opposite direction. It can be refilled with any liquid.
  - <span style="color:cyan">Grappling Gun</span>: A movement tool with infinite potential. It's fired like any other gun, and can be retracted by reloading. Once you hook onto something, click <span style="color:yellow">**[keybind="UseSecondary"]**</span> to reel yourself in.
  - <span style="color:cyan">Salvage PDA</span>: Your PDA spawns with the AstroNav GPS program installed, allowing you to use it as a GPS when held in hand. Be sure to note the station's coordinates before you head out!
  - <span style="color:cyan">Mass Scanner Computer</span>: A computer console which provides a top-down view of the station and nearby entities in space. Use it to double-check the location and direction of debris or asteroids before you travel to them.

  
    [[实体:RadioHandheld]]
    [[实体:HandheldGPSBasic]]
    [[实体:ClothingBeltUtilityFilled]]
  

  Finally, there are also some helpful but not required equipment that is available to Salvage.

  - <span style="color:cyan">Handheld Radio</span>: This lets you communicate with other Salvage Specialists even if regular radio communications are unavailable.
  - <span style="color:cyan">Global Positioning System</span>: A spare GPS system, identical to the one pre-installed in your PDA. Useful as a backup option or as equipment from newly-hired Salvagers.
  - <span style="color:cyan">Utility Belt</span>: This holds all the equipment you will need to loot machines and the like from Salvage Wrecks and the Mining Asteroid. Wear it anytime you aren't planning on mining.

  ## Salvage Magnet Pulls

  Once prepared, it is time to go and explore the stars.

  
    [[实体:SalvageMagnet]]
  

  The <span style="color:cyan">Salvage Magnet</span> is the machine that brings space debris into nearby space.

  The Salvage Magnet provides five offers: they will be a random mix of <span style="color:cyan">Asteroids</span>, <span style="color:cyan">Salvage Wrecks</span>, and <span style="color:cyan">Space Debris</span>. After choosing one of the offers, the selected chunk will be pulled in for the next **6 minutes**. At the end of this time, any loot remaining on the pulled grid will disappear. If none of the offers look appealing to you, they refresh every **3 minutes** if unclaimed.

  During the 6 minutes, fly to the pulled grid, extract all profit possible, and then fly back to the station.

### Asteroids

  
    [[实体:Pickaxe]]
    [[实体:OreBag]]
    [[实体:OreProcessor]]
  

  The Salvage Magnet provides information on the types of resources on an asteroid, which can help you decide which to prioritize based on the current needs of the station. This is a relatively safe option for mining as there are no hostile creatures on asteroids - though there are certainly still threats...

  All you need to do is fly around and mine the ore with your <span style="color:cyan">Pickaxe</span> and <span style="color:cyan">Ore Bag</span>. Once you have collected all the ore, you can refine it into materials through the <span style="color:cyan">Ore Processor</span>.

  Tread lightly - hidden in seemingly normal rock, chunks of <span style="color:red">Gibtonite</span> hide, ready to explode at the slightest disturbance.

### Space Debris

  
    [[实体:Wrench]]
    [[实体:FireExtinguisher]]
    [[实体:Recycler]]
  

  <span style="color:cyan">Space Debris</span> is a versatile option, providing vital basic materials (such as steel, glass, etc.) in short supply at the start of the shift. Items and scrap are scattered across the debris, and must be collected. Consider dragging along one of the lockers or crates aboard the debris for easier storage.

  Debris is sprinkled with numerous threats. Bloodthirsty <span style="color:red">Space Carp</span> and <span style="color:red">Sharkminnows</span> infest them, eager to rush any Salvagers that come nearby. <span style="color:red">Landmines</span> are also rarely found aboard debris, capable of incapacitating the unwary explorer.

  Once you return from the Debris, place any scrap you found aboard the debris into your department's <span style="color:cyan">Recycler</span> to transform it into useful materials and give it to Cargo to distribute to the station. Should you find valuables aboard the Debris, like <span style="color:yellow">Coins, Rings, and Watches,</span> give them to Cargo so they can be sold at the <span style="color:cyan">Automated Trade Station</span> for profit.

  ### Salvage Wrecks

  
    [[实体:SurvivalKnife]]
    [[实体:Fulton]]
    [[实体:WeaponProtoKineticAccelerator]]
  

  The Salvage Magnet provides information on the size of a Wreck. Salvage Wrecks vary wildly in both threat and value, but they often have more diverse threats than <span style="color:cyan">Space Debris</span>. Keep your distance from hostiles and shoot at them with the <span style="color:cyan">Proto-Kinetic Accelerator</span>. Look out for valuable loot and machinery, and use your <span style="color:cyan">Appraisal Tool</span> to check the value of different things.

  ## Progression & Upgrades

  While your starting equipment is all you need to do your job at a basic level, advanced gear makes your work far easier, and gives you the equipment necessary to let you tackle the dreaded <span style="color:red">Mining Asteroid</span>

  ### Salvage Job Board

  
    [[实体:ComputerSalvageJobBoard]]
  

  The <span style="color:cyan">Salvage Job Board</span> is a computer located in the Salvage Bay. It serves as Salvage's version of the <span style="color:cyan">Cargo Bounty Computer</span>, allowing Salvagers to fulfill specific jobs with Salvage-specific materials in order to earn Spesos and unlock <span style="color:yellow">Advanced Salvage Gear</span>.

  Once you gather the supplies necessary to complete a job, print out a <span style="color:cyan">Salvage Job Shipment Label</span> and attach it to the crate or locker containing the goods. You can use your <span style="color:cyan">Appraisal Tool</span> to double-check that it's ready for sale. The prepared bounty can then be brought to the <span style="color:cyan">Automated Trade Station</span> to earn Cargo Spesos and to unlock new Salvage-specific crates in the <span style="color:cyan">Cargo Request Computer</span>.

  
    [[实体:Fulton1]]
    [[实体:WeaponCrusher]]
    [[实体:SeismicCharge]]
  

  Completing Salvage Jobs will upgrade your rank and unlock new equipment purchases in the <span style="color:cyan">Cargo Request Computer</span>, including:

  - <span style="color:cyan">Fultons</span>: Once a Fulton has been linked to a Fulton Beacon, they can be attached to unanchored structures like crates or machines to teleport them back to the Beacon after a short wait. Exceptionally useful for moving both items and people.
  - <span style="color:cyan">Crusher Weapons</span>: A series of upgraded melee options for Salvagers to purchase. The <span style="color:red">Crusher Dagger</span> is an upgrade to your Survival knife, swinging automatically and far faster than a regular knife. The <span style="color:red">Crusher</span> and upgraded <span style="color:red">Crusher Glaive</span> are gigantic polearms which can fire special projectiles when wielded. These projectiles do no damage on their own, but if a marked enemy is meleed quickly enough, you'll leech some of their life away to heal yourself. All Crusher weaponry comes with a built-in floodlamp.
  - <span style="color:cyan">Seismic Charges</span>: Mining explosives intended to be used to break up asteroids or breach into dangerous wrecks. Remember that you can link them to a remote detonator or set them to start detonating when attached to a wall for safer demolition.

  ### Working with Science

  
    [[实体:ToyFigurineScientist]]
    [[实体:ResearchDisk]]
  

  The <span style="color:purple">Science Department</span> is a crucial partner for Salvage. Science depends on Salvage for both basic materials (steel, plastic, glass) and advanced materials (silver, gold, plasma, and uranium), and Salvage relies on Science for the upgraded equipment that makes their work easier.

  Whenever possible, communicate with Science and try to prioritize their requests. While Science will need large quantities of basic materials, they rely entirely on you for their supply of <span style="color:yellow">Gold, Silver and Uranium,</span> all of which are required for your advanced gear. If you want Science to help you, you have to help them - and vice versa.

  
    [[实体:MiningDrill]]
    [[实体:HandHeldMassScanner]]
    [[实体:PKAUpgradeRange]]
  

  Science is the only source for a variety of extremely useful gear, including:

  - <span style="color:cyan">Mining Gear</span>: Science can provide you with <span style="color:yellow">Mining Drills and Mineral Scanners</span>, which make mining faster and easier. They can also build you an <span style="color:yellow">Industrial Ore Processor</span>, which processes ore into finished materials at a much better rate.
  - <span style="color:cyan">Handheld Mass Scanner</span>: A pocket-portable Mass Scanner Computer. Though it requires a battery to use, it lets you view your position anywhere in space.
  - <span style="color:cyan">PKA Upgrades</span>: You can upgrade your PKA with up to two unique upgrades from a pool of three: <span style="color:red">Damage,</span> <span style="color:yellow">Fire Rate,</span> and <span style="color:green">Range</span>.

  ## The Mining Asteroid

  Once you feel that you've mastered the Salvage Magnet, the distant <span style="color:red">Mining Asteroid</span> serves as the next step in a Salvager's journey. Reaching the Mining Asteroid generally requires a shuttle or jetpacks, and it should generally not be explored alone due to the exceptional dangers within.

  
    [[实体:MobGoliath]]
    [[实体:MobHivelord]]
    [[实体:MobBasilisk]]
  

  The Mining Asteroid consists of two sections: the <span style="color:cyan">Dungeon</span>, a randomly-generated structure filled with enemies and material loot, and the <span style="color:cyan">Asteroid</span>, which contains vast deposits of mineral wealth dotted with pockets of loot and monsters.

  Beware of the monsters aboard the Mining Asteroid, as they stand head and shoulders above the threats present on Wrecks and Debris.

  - <span style="color:cyan">Goliaths</span>: Titanic beasts which can incapacitate targets with writhing tendrils, leaving them helpless against their massive claws and gnashing teeth. They're a pain to take down, but they can be butchered for valuable <span style="color:cyan">Goliath Hide Plating,</span> useful in both completing Salvage Jobs and upgrading your Spationaut Suit.
  - <span style="color:cyan">Hivelords</span>: Fast moving and erratic anomalies. They prefer to keep their distance in combat, spewing out small <span style="color:cyan">Hivelord Brood</span> to swarm Salvagers. They're not especially tough, so ambush them quickly before they can summon a horde to drown you in. They can be butchered for the quick-rotting <span style="color:cyan">Hivelord Cores,</span> which are used to complete Salvage Jobs and can also be eaten raw for quick healing.
  - <span style="color:cyan">Basilisks</span>: An ice-encrusted terror that lurks in the shadows. Basilisks can fire freezing energy bolts which damage and slow their targets, and will often use them to soften up a target before moving in for the kill. Once defeated, they can be butchered for invaluable <span style="color:cyan">Diamond Ore</span>.
