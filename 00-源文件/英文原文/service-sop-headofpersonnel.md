# Head of Personnel

[[实体:ToyFigurineHeadOfPersonnel]]

1. The Head of Personnel should not grant access to a department unless they have a form stamped by the relevant head of department.

2. The Head of Personnel should not do the work of other departments unless they are short staffed.

3. The Head of Personnel may not hire any staff to a NanoTrasen Personnel position.
