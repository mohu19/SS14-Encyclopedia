# Robotics
  The science team has the ability to create several unique types of robots to suit the station's needs. In order to begin, you'll want to research the <span style="color:#a4885c">Robotics</span> technology.

  ## Exosuit Fabricator

[[实体:ExosuitFabricator]]

  The <span style="color:#a4885c">Exosuit Fabricator</span> is where you'll be printing out most of the parts for your creations, such as:
  - [textlink="Cyborg" link="Cyborgs"] limbs
  - Mech parts and equipment
  - Proximity sensors

  Operating the exosuit fabricator is the same as every other lathe on the station; simply click the fabricator to open the UI, then click the icon for whatever part you want to print.
  - Are there no recipes? click the <span style="color:#a4885c">sync</span> button on the UI to check for any new blueprints. If, even then, nothing shows up, you've likely not researched the relevant technology yet!
  - If a recipe appears grayed-out, it's likely due to a lack of materials. To insert new materials (either <span style="color:#99A3A4">steel</span>, <span style="color:#ECF0F1">plastic</span>, or <span style="color:#AED6F1">glass</span>), simply click the fabricator while holding said material.

  Every station starts with a full suite of robotics equipment by default, but you can make another fabricator if <span style="color:#a4885c">Robotics</span> technology is available, which will allow you to make the respective circuit board.

  ## Bots

[[实体:MobCleanBot]]

[[实体:MobHonkBot]]
[[实体:MobMimeBot]]

  <span style="color:#a4885c">Bots</span> are small, simple automata which can perform simple tasks, such as cleaning. They don't use power cells, but are quite fragile. Making these will require parts which can be made at an exosuit fabricator.

  To make a bot, open the <span style="color:#a4885c">Construction Menu</span> and go to the "Utilities" category (or just search "bot") and you'll have the whole list of recipes. In order to assemble the bot, you must have the parts outlined by the recipe close to you and click <span style="color:#a4885c">Build</span>, after a few seconds your newly created silicon servant should appear at your feet!

  ## Mechs

[[实体:MechRipley]]
[[实体:MechHonker]]
[[实体:MechHamtr]]

  <span style="color:#a4885c">Mechs</span> are giant behemoths of metal designed to carry out a variety of functions respective to their model. They are piloted by players and require power cells to function.

  Each mech requires its own unique set of limbs and parts to create. These can be fabricated at the Exosuit Fabricator, the same as any other part, save for the required <span style="color:#a4885c">circuit boards</span>, which are made at a <span style="color:#a4885c">Circuit Board Imprinter</span>, which can normally be found in the [textlink="science" link="Science"] department.

  To start making a mech, print their harness in the fabricator and insert the limbs, you can <span style="color:#a4885c">examine</span> it to see how to proceed with construction once all limbs are in place.
  - Every mech requires some combination of wires, tool usage, and their own unique circuit boards to construct after the chassis has been assembled. Most mechs have two or more unique circuit boards. For example, the <span style="color:#a4885c">Ripley</span> mech requires a <span style="color:#a4885c">ripley controls module</span> board and a <span style="color:#a4885c">ripley peripherals module</span> board.
  - Mechs require large amounts of material to create - be sure to ask your fellow scientists and cargo personnel for any materials you're missing!
  - The Vim exosuit requires a pair of borg legs and an EVA helmet, regular or syndicate, then a voice trigger and a power cell.

  ## Equipment
    A mech is nothing more than an expensive hunk of metal without their exclusive tools. Equipment can be created at the fabricator, the same as any other part.

[[实体:MechEquipmentGrabber]]

  - To install equipment, just click on the mech with the equipment in your hand.
  - To use equipment, perform the "Cycle Equipment" action until the tool you desire is equipped.
