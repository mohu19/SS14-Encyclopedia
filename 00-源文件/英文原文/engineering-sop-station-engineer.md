# Station Engineer

[[实体:ToyFigurineEngineer]]

1. Station Engineers report directly to the Chief Engineer.

2. Station Engineers must construct the AME and/or solar network before moving onto permanent station engines.

3. Station Engineers must establish permanent station power before moving on to other duties or confirm Atmospheric Technicians have established permanent power.

4. Station Engineers must check the singularity, tesla and power monitoring computer every 30 minutes or less.

5. It is recommended that at least one Station Engineer remains in the department during non-emergencies to assist other crew members and respond to potential department emergencies.
