# Vulpkanin

  
    [[实体:AppearanceVulpkanin]]
  

  Vulpkanin, due to their dense fur, <span style="color:#1e90ff">prefer colder temperatures</span> and <span style="color:#ffa500">heat up faster.</span>

  Their diet allows them to safely eat raw meat but they get poisoned by theobromine.

  Their weirdly shaped muzzle leads to difficulties drinking, making them sometimes spill small amounts of whatever they drank onto the ground.

  They take <span style="color:#1e90ff">15% less Cold damage</span> but <span style="color:#ffa500">15% more Heat damage.</span>.

  Their eyesight allows them to see slightly further than most species.

  They have a faster metabolism than other species. This makes them hungrier, but it also means that they can process chemicals 25% faster then a standard human.
