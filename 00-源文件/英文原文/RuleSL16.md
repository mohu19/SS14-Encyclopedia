# Rule 16 - End-of-Round Griefing
The round continues after the results screen. Neither crew nor antags should engage in any form of violence or griefing at this point without in-character justification.
In the case of Zombies, Nuclear/Lone Operatives, or other cases when this rule is suspended, an admin will remove pacification and make it known.
\n - [color=000000] Defending yourself is an example of in-character justification, as is sec apprehending known criminals. [/color]
\n - [color=000000] Antags cannot complete their goals after the results screen, and so should not seek out any targets.[/color] [bold]<span style="color:red">RED MEANS YOU FAILED, IT’S TOO LATE.</span>
\n - [color=000000] Breaking things, Hacking/Emagging and attempting to trespass into the Command briefing in Central Command is not allowed without proper in-character justification. Non-Command trespassers may be executed if they refuse to leave without a valid reason.[/color]
\n - [color=000000] Disrupting wind-down roleplay using non-lethal measures such as metal foam grenades, lube grenades/foam.[/color]
\n - [color=000000] Stealing items from other players.  ERT/NTSF working a checkpoint, or security confiscating contraband is excluded from this.[/color]
