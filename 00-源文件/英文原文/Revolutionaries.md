# Revolutionaries / Red Tide

  
    <span style="color:#999999">*"URAAHH!"*</span>
  
  
    [[实体:BannerSovietHammer]]
  

  Revolutionaries are conversion antagonists hired by the <span style="color:yellow">Union of Soviet Socialist Planets</span>, who are tasked with taking control over any of the stations, including <span style="color:#5346e0">yours</span>.

  ## Objectives
  You must convert, cuff or kill all of the [textlink="Command staff" link="Command"] on station in no particular order.

  Your objective is **not to destroy the station**, but *to take it over*, so try to minimize damage where possible.

  **The revolution will fail** if all of the **<span style="color:#ed7300">Head Revolutionaries</span>** die, and all <span style="color:red">Revolutionaries</span> will de-convert if this happens.

  ## Headrevs & Conversion
  **<span style="color:#ed7300">Head Revolutionaries</span>** are chosen at the start of the shift and begin with a <span style="color:cyan">flash</span>, a pair of <span style="color:cyan">sunglasses</span>, and an <span style="color:#ed7300">SKB uplink implanter</span>.

  
    [[实体:Flash]]
    [[实体:ClothingEyesGlassesSunglasses]]
    [[实体:USSPUplinkImplanter]]
  

  You can convert crew members to your side by taking any **flash** and attacking someone with it using <span style="color:#ff0000">harm mode</span>.
  However, **you can't convert your target if they're wearing <span style="color:cyan">flash protection</span>** such as sunglasses, welding mask/goggles, or if they're a character with blind trait.

  
    [[实体:ClothingEyesGlassesSunglasses]]
    [[实体:ClothingEyesGlassesSecurity]]
    [[实体:ClothingHeadHatWelding]]
    [[实体:ClothingEyesWeldingGoggles]]
  

  **<span style="color:red">Revolutionaries</span> can't convert people themselves,** but they're more than capable of doing dirty work, carrying out orders, or distribute supplies if **they were implanted with <span style="color:#ed7300">Head Revolutionary's SKB uplink.</span>**

  ## MindShields™

  Another obstacle in your way are those pesky brainwashing <span style="color:cyan">MindShield™ implanters</span>.

  
    [[实体:MindShieldImplanter]]
  

  These will:
  - Prevent the implanted person from being converted into a <span style="color:red">Revolutionary</span>
  - De-convert Revolutionaries, and will make them no longer loyal to your cause
  - **Visibly be destroyed upon being implanted into a <span style="color:#ed7300">Head Revolutionary</span>**, giving you away
  - NOT protect against flash disorientation

  Assume all of <span style="color:#cb0000">Security</span>, <span style="color:#1b67a5">Command</span>, <span style="color:#a86819">Salvage specialists</span>, <span style="color:#5b97bc">Paramedics</span> and <span style="color:#478bff">Internal Affairs Agents</span> to be implanted with <span style="color:cyan">MindShields™</span> already, **however they can be removed by using an empty implanter obtainable from the Medical department's MedFab, or by using an <span style="color:#ed7300">[bold]SKB Demindshielder**</span> available in your <span style="color:#ed7300">uplink.</span>[/bold] 

  
    [[实体:ToyFigurineSecurity]]
    [[实体:ToyFigurineCaptain]]  
    [[实体:ToyFigurineSalvage]]
    [[实体:ToyFigurineLawyer]]

  ## Svyaz-UZL uplink (Telebonds & Total Conversion)

  <span style="color:#ed7300">Head Revolutionaries</span> start additionally with an <span style="color:#ed7300">SKB uplink implanter</span>, which can be implanted into them or in a <span style="color:red">Revolutionary</span> **converted by them specifically.**

  
    [[实体:USSPUplinkImplanter]]
    [[实体:BaseRevUplinkRadio]]
  

  The subdermal uplink allows purchase of various **global limited stocks** of weapons and tools for the revolution via **<span style="color:#5c5c5c">Telebonds</span>** and **<span style="color:#e3a736">Total Conversion.</span>**

  - **<span style="color:#5c5c5c">Telebonds</span>**, much like <span style="color:red">Telecrystals</span> are a individual private currency obtainable from **conversion**, that are each specific to each <span style="color:#ed7300">Head Revolutionary</span>.
  - **<span style="color:#e3a736">Total Conversion</span>**, apart from showing how many crew members have been converted, is a **score shared among all <span style="color:#ed7300">Head Revolutionaries</span>**. Which are mainly used in the **GLOBAL** section of the uplink, allowing you to place things like the <span style="color:yellow">supply rift</span>.

  When a **<span style="color:#ed7300">Head Revolutionary</span>** converts someone, they gain **<span style="color:#5c5c5c">1 Telebond</span>** privately, and **<span style="color:#e3a736">1 Total Conversion</span>** globally.

  Remember, **<span style="color:#e3a736">Total Conversion</span>** is a **global progress score, not a currency**, allowing you to gain items for free as soon as you reach certain conversions.

  The uplink stocks are shared among all who use the uplink, and additionally it shows who has last purchased the item.

  
    [[实体:ClothingEyesGlassesSKBGlasses]]
    [[实体:RevolutionaryHeldBanner]]
    [[实体:EncryptionKeySoviet]]
  

  ## Supply Rift

  Upon reaching **20 total conversions**, you will be able to place a <span style="color:yellow">supply rift</span>, which will drop soviet supplies every 35 seconds.

  
    [[实体:RevSupplyRift]]
    [[实体:CrateSovietArmaments]]
    [[实体:WeaponSniperMosin]]
  

  The <span style="color:yellow">rift</span> will **secretly inform all <span style="color:red">revolutionaries</span> in the chat of it's location** upon spawn.

  At 50% charge, the station **will be alerted of an energy influx**, thus **exposing you** at the cost of infinite supplies in the long run.

  If a <span style="color:yellow">rift</span> is destroyed, you won't be able to place another one. And you'll only be able to place another if the **current one is charged.**

  It is recommended to discuss early on in the round where you'll place the <span style="color:yellow">rift</span> with your <span style="color:red">revolutionaries</span> so that they can **fortify** the area to prevent <span style="color:#cb0000">Security</span> from **destroying** it.

  ## Tips

  As a <span style="color:#ed7300">Head Revolutionary</span>, it's recommended that:
  - You **convert** people away from public spaces. Apart from being potentially seen by the <span style="color:#70a7ff">Station AI</span>, flash and knockdowns are loud and it can be heard by anyone not loyal to you. Out of public conversions are best done by a <span style="color:blue">Paramedic</span>.

  
    [[实体:StationAiHolo]]
    [[实体:ToyFigurineParamedic]]
  

  - It's very beneficial to start as one or convert <span style="color:#f5be51">Cargo/Mail Technicians</span> and <span style="color:#a319ff">Scientists</span>, due to their sufficient ability to **stop incoming orders of <span style="color:cyan">MindShields™</span>** or to **provide you with flashes made from an <span style="color:blue">Exosuit Fabricator</span>**, respectively.

  
    [[实体:ToyFigurineCargoTech]]
    [[实体:ToyFigurineScientist]]
    [[实体:ExosuitFabricator]]
  

  - Be careful who you're trying to convert. Early on in the round some crew like <span style="color:green">Musicians</span> or <span style="color:green">Bartenders</span> wear **sunglasses**. While <span style="color:#a86819">Salvage specialists</span> are outright <span style="color:cyan">MindShielded™</span>.

  
    [[实体:ToyFigurineMusician]]
    [[实体:ToyFigurineBartender]]
    [[实体:ToyFigurineSalvage]]
  

  - If you're in for a long run, convert <span style="color:#2189ff">Medical</span> personnel. Long revolutionary rounds will inevitably mean lots of losses. Competent doctors will prioritize your health and of your allies over those from <span style="color:#cb0000">Security</span> or <span style="color:#1b67a5">Command</span>, if by any chance they're getting treated in <span style="color:#2189ff">Medbay</span> rather than in <span style="color:#cb0000">Brigmedic's medical bay</span>.

  
    [[实体:ToyFigurineMedicalDoctor]]
    [[实体:ToyFigurineChemist]]
  

  - **<span style="color:#5c5c5c">Telebonds</span>** can be withdrawn and shared among all who use the <span style="color:#ed7300">uplink</span>. As such, if you want to purchase **one of the more expensive items**, it's best to meet with your <span style="color:#ed7300">Head Revolutionaries</span> later on in the round if no prior **<span style="color:#5c5c5c">Telebonds</span>** purchases were made.

  
    [[实体:Telebond]]
    [[实体:WeaponLightMachineGunDP28]]
    [[实体:WeaponSubMachineGunPPSH]]
    [[实体:ClothingOuterHardsuitSovietMarine]]
  

  As a <span style="color:red">Revolutionary</span>, it's recommended that:

  - You protect your <span style="color:#ed7300">Head Revolutionary</span> at all costs. Protect them from <span style="color:red">the law</span>, by trading IDs and goods so that they can make their escapes.

  
    [[实体:ComputerCriminalRecords]]
    [[实体:CargoIDCard]]
  

  - If a <span style="color:yellow">supply rift</span> was placed, you **will be secretly informed of it's location**. So do your best and provide help when possible.

  
    [[实体:DefaultStationBeaconBar]]
  

  - If you know where the <span style="color:#ed7300">Head Revolutionaries</span> will place the <span style="color:yellow">rift</span> beforehand, you can help reinforcing the area.

  
    [[实体:Table]]
    [[实体:WallSolid]]
    [[实体:VendingMachineBooze]]
  

  - And lastly, have fun. As long as it helps the revolution without severly damaging the station.
