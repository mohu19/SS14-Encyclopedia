  The Construction Menu is how you build and craft objects in the game.
  It can be accessed by pressing <span style="color:yellow">**[keybind="OpenCraftingMenu"]**</span>.

  When placing objects that "snap" to the grid, you can hold <span style="color:yellow">**[keybind="EditorLinePlace"]**</span> to place an entire line at a time, and <span style="color:yellow">**[keybind="EditorGridPlace"]**</span> to place an entire grid at a time.

  When crafting objects with a lot of ingredients, keep in mind you don't have to hold everything at once; you can simply place the ingredients on the floor, in your backpack or on a table near you, and they'll be used up during crafting like normal.

  When placing a "building ghost" somewhere in the world, press <span style="color:yellow">**[keybind="EditorRotateObject"]**</span> to rotate the ghost clockwise.
  If you are building a mirrorable component (think: Gas Mixers/Filters), you can press <span style="color:yellow">**[keybind="EditorFlipObject"]**</span> to flip the ghost.

  You can also use <span style="color:yellow">**[keybind="RotateObjectClockwise"]**</span> and <span style="color:yellow">**[keybind="RotateObjectCounterclockwise"]**</span> to rotate an object clockwise or counterclockwise if it's already built, as long as it is unanchored (unwrenched).
